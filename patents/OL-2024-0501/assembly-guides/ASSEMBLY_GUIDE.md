## II. Step-by-Step Assembly
### Step 1: Harvesting the Pulse
Disassemble the utility lighter. You are looking for the small, rectangular plastic module with a spring-loaded button and a single insulated wire.
 * **Scientific Note:** The "ground" for the piezo is often the metal casing of the lighter. You must solder or tightly wrap a second copper lead to the small metal tab at the base of the piezo unit to complete the circuit.
### Step 2: Preparing the Conductors
Cut two lengths of copper wire (approx. 3 inches each). Strip 5mm of insulation from both ends.
 * **The Sharpness Principle:** File the tips of the "Gap" ends to a sharp point. According to **Lightning Physics**, charge accumulates at sharp points (the "Power of Points"), making it easier to ionize the air and initiate the spark.
### Step 3: Housing the Plasma Channel
Slide your conductors into the PVC housing from opposite ends.
 * **The Gap Calibration:** Position the tips so they are exactly **1.5mm to 2mm** apart.
 * **Insulation Check:** Use the epoxy to seal the wires into the PVC. This prevents the high-voltage pulse from tracking back to your fingers (the "path of least resistance").

 * **MHD Interaction:** When the spark fires, the magnetic field exerts a force on the moving ions. This expands the plasma volume, increasing the surface area for **Ozone (O_3) production**.
