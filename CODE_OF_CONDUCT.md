# OMEGA Code of Conduct

We are sovereign individuals building together. Mutual respect is required. Gatekeeping, harassment, and extraction are not welcome.

---

## Our Commitment

The Scientist Gem | OMEGA Protocol is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all contributors, regardless of background, experience level, perspective, or identity.

We believe that scientific discovery thrives when diverse voices collaborate freely and ethically.

---

## Core Values

- **Sovereignty**: Each individual's autonomy and right to contribute is respected
- **Reciprocity**: We give and receive in equal measure; extraction without contribution is not tolerated
- **Integrity**: Honesty, transparency, and attribution are non-negotiable
- **Collaboration**: We work together toward shared goals while respecting differences
- **Ethical Practice**: All tools and knowledge are applied responsibly for universal benefit

---

## Expected Behavior

By participating in this community, you agree to:

### Respect & Dignity
- Treat all contributors with respect and professionalism
- Welcome diverse perspectives and approaches
- Engage in good-faith discussion and debate
- Listen actively and seek to understand different viewpoints

### Attribution & Integrity
- Provide proper credit to original creators and contributors
- Acknowledge sources and references
- Avoid plagiarism or misrepresentation of work
- Be transparent about your contributions and limitations

### Ethical Collaboration
- Contribute with genuine intent to advance the science
- Share knowledge freely without gatekeeping
- Support others' growth and learning
- Maintain confidentiality when requested

### Constructive Feedback
- Provide thoughtful, actionable feedback
- Focus on ideas, not personalities
- Assume good intent in others' contributions
- Help improve work rather than tear it down

### Responsible Use
- Apply tools and knowledge ethically and responsibly
- Consider legal and safety implications
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations
- Contribute to a culture of responsible innovation

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

### Harassment & Discrimination
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory language
- Discrimination based on identity, background, or perspective
- Unwelcome sexual advances or comments
- Intimidation, threats, or bullying
- Deliberate exclusion or gatekeeping

### Dishonesty & Extraction
- Plagiarism or misattribution of work
- Unauthorized use of others' contributions
- Commercial extraction without reciprocity
- Deliberate misinformation or scientific misconduct
- Misrepresentation of credentials or authority

### Harmful Use
- Attempts to weaponize or misuse scientific tools
- Violation of laws or regulations
- Disregard for safety protocols
- Deliberate harm to individuals or communities

### Disruptive Behavior
- Spam or off-topic content
- Deliberate disruption of discussions
- Manipulation or bad-faith arguments
- Attempts to derail productive collaboration

## Reporting & Resolution

### How to Report

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document the incident** - Note what happened, when, and who was involved
2. **Report to maintainers** - Contact the project maintainer directly via email or private message
3. **Provide context** - Include relevant details to help understand the situation
4. **Respect confidentiality** - Your report will be handled with discretion

### Investigation & Response

Upon receiving a report:

1. **Initial Review** - Maintainers will assess the report and gather additional context
2. **Investigation** - A fair and thorough investigation will be conducted
3. **Communication** - Both parties will be informed of findings and next steps
4. **Resolution** - Appropriate action will be taken based on findings

### Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- **Warning**: First offense; clear explanation of violation and expectations
- **Temporary Suspension**: Repeated violations; removal from community for a defined period
- **Permanent Ban**: Severe or repeated violations; permanent removal from the community

**Maintainer decision is final.** We reserve the right to take action at our discretion to protect the community.
